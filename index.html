Why documents matter

What happens to what you send

It gets you paid

An invoice from their own shopper portal, plus an email approving your work, is most of what a small claims judge needs. Contractors who have documents win. We give yours back to you organized and ready to file.

It establishes a pattern

One unpaid invoice is a billing dispute. Eighty-six unpaid invoices across twenty-five states, over six years, with the same explanations repeated to different people, is something else. Regulators respond to the second thing.

It reaches the people who hire them

Elite CXS is paid by client companies to run their shopper programs. Several of those contracts require that shoppers actually be paid. With your permission, we take documented claims directly to the client you shopped for.

Nothing is published without your permission. The upload form asks separately what you allow: private use only, sharing with regulators and client companies, or listing your name publicly. Choose one, all, or none — and change your mind at any time by emailing us.

Worth knowing

This has worked before

One contractor took Elite CXS to small claims court in Pasco County, Florida, where the company is based, and won a judgment. Payment came only after a motion for contempt was filed — but it came. The case number is 51-2025-SC-004786-SCAX-WS and it is public record.

Most of the claims here are between $25 and $600. That is squarely small claims territory in every state, and you do not need a lawyer to file. The playbook walks through it.
