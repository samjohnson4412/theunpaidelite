Step two — the one that matters most

Send your documents

A claim says money is owed. A document proves it. This is the single most useful thing you can do, both for your own recovery and for everyone else’s.

Go to the upload form ↓

What to send

Send whatever you have. Incomplete is fine — a single screenshot is better than nothing, and people often have more than they think.

Most valuable

  • Invoices from their shopper portal — showing the amount and the date it was due. Several people have these and don’t realize how much they matter.
  • Assignment confirmations — the email or page confirming you were assigned the shop, especially if it states the fee and the reimbursement.
  • Emails from Elite CXS about payment — above all the ones promising payment, blaming a processor change, or saying it was escalated.

Also useful

  • Receipts for purchases you made out of pocket
  • Screenshots of your completed and approved shop reports
  • Your own record of calls — dates, numbers, who you spoke to
  • Any 1099 you received from them
  • Texts or messages from schedulers or recruiters

A note on screenshots

If you still have access to their shopper portal, take screenshots now. Several contractors have reported losing access. Capture your invoice list, each invoice, and your completed shop history while you can.

You decide how yours is used

The form asks three separate permission questions. They are independent — say yes to one, all, or none, and your documents are still welcome.

Private use only

Your documents are used only to understand the pattern and to help you personally. They go nowhere else. This is the default, and it is a completely reasonable choice.

Share with regulators

Allows us to include your documents in complaints to attorneys general, state labor agencies and law enforcement, where a pattern across many people carries real weight.

Share with the client you shopped for

Allows us to show the company whose locations you shopped that the shopper it paid for was never paid. Some of those contracts require it. This is the fastest lever we have.

You can change your mind at any time by emailing sam@crossroadstechnology.co. Permission is withdrawn from that point forward — we cannot recall a complaint already filed, but nothing further will be shared and your documents will be removed on request.

Redact freely. Black out your bank details, Social Security number and home address before uploading. Nothing we do needs them. If a document only exists unredacted, send it and say so, and we will redact it before it goes anywhere.

Upload

No account or sign-in required. You can upload multiple files at once, and phone photos of paper documents are fine.

Can’t use the form?

Email your documents to sam@crossroadstechnology.co with a line saying how we may use them. That works just as well — the form is only there to keep things organized.
