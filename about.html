What this project does

  • Collects claims and documents in one place so the scale is visible
  • Publishes the company’s verified details and what other contractors have tried, so nobody has to work it out alone
  • Refers the documented pattern to regulators and prosecutors, with permission
  • Takes documented claims to the client companies that hired Elite CXS, with permission
  • Keeps a public, sourced record of what happened

What it does not do

  • It is not a class action. There is no lawsuit to join, no settlement pending, and no attorney of record. Every claim here belongs to the person who filed it, and every decision about pursuing it is theirs alone. Nothing is ever filed in your name.
  • It does not give legal advice. Nobody here is an attorney. We can tell you what other contractors did and how it went. We cannot tell you what you should do, and we cannot represent you. If your claim is large or your filing deadline is close, talk to a lawyer — and we will give you whatever we have that helps.
  • It does not take your money. There is no fee, no donation link and no cut of anything you recover. If anyone contacts you claiming to collect on behalf of this site, it is not us — tell us immediately.
  • It does not speak for you. Nothing identifying you is shared outside this group unless you have specifically allowed it.

Your information

Plain terms, no policy document:

  • Contact details are used to reach you about this effort. Nothing else. They are never sold and never published.
  • Your name appears publicly only if you asked for it to.
  • Documents you send are used only in the ways you permitted on the upload form.
  • This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no trackers. We do not know who visits it.
  • The forms and file storage are operated by outside providers, whose own privacy terms apply to what passes through them.
  • Ask us to delete your information and we delete it. You do not have to give a reason.

Corrections

This site makes factual claims about a named company, so it needs to be accurate, and that includes being correctable.

If anything here is wrong — a number, a characterization, a detail about the company — email sam@crossroadstechnology.co. We will check it against the underlying claim forms and documents. If it is wrong we will fix it and note the change. That applies to anyone, including Elite CXS or anyone associated with it.

Sourcing

Statements about what contractors experienced are attributed to contractors, because that is what they are: accounts given by people who filled out a claim form. Statements about court filings refer to public records, identified by case number. Where we do not know something, the site says so.
