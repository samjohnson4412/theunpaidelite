File a claim

Five minutes. It puts you on the list, it adds your amount to the total, and it is how we reach you. You don’t need to gather anything first — once we see your claim, we’ll tell you what would actually help it.

Already filled this out?

If you submitted this form any time since July 2025, you are already counted and you do not need to file again. Send your documents when you are ready, or just wait — we are working through the list and will come to you.

What you’ll be asked

  • Your name and how to reach you
  • Where you performed the shops
  • What you are owed — unpaid fees plus anything you bought with your own money
  • The dates you worked
  • What happened when you tried to get paid

Estimates are fine. If you can’t remember an exact amount or date, put your best guess and say so — an approximate claim on the list is far more useful than a perfect one you never file. The form only accepts one date, so if you worked several, put the most recent one in the date field and list the rest in the last question.

Opens in a new tab. No account or sign-in needed. The form is hosted on Google Forms and the answers go to a spreadsheet only we can see — not to Elite CXS, not to anyone else. If it won’t work for you, email sam@crossroadstechnology.co with what you’re owed and where you shopped instead; that works just as well.

What happens after you file

  1. We read your claim. Every one, individually.
  2. We come back to you with what would help. This is the part that matters. What is useful depends entirely on your situation — whether you still have portal access, which state you worked in, how old the work is, whether you got a 1099, how much is at stake. We tell you what to look for rather than handing you a generic checklist and hoping.
  3. Your amount joins the total on the record, anonymously unless you ask otherwise.
  4. You get the recovery tools — the small claims walkthrough, complaint language, and the filing details for your state.

If you already know what you have and would rather not wait, send it now.

What we do with your information

Your email address and phone number are used to contact you about this effort and nothing else. They are never published, never sold, and never shared with anyone outside this group without you specifically allowing it. Your name appears publicly only if you ask us to list it.

Want your information removed? Email sam@crossroadstechnology.co and it is deleted. No questions, no delay.
