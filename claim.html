Step one

File a claim

Five minutes. It puts you on the list, it adds your amount to the total, and it is how we reach you when something happens.

Already filled out the older Google form?

You are already counted — you do not need to file again. What we need from you now is your paperwork. Send your documents here.

What you’ll be asked

  • Your name and how to reach you
  • Where you performed the shops, and for which client if you know it
  • What you are owed — unpaid fees plus anything you bought with your own money
  • The dates you worked
  • What happened when you tried to get paid

Estimates are fine. If you are not sure of an exact amount or date, say so on the form and send what records you have later.

What happens next

  1. You get a confirmation with a copy of what you submitted.
  2. We ask for your documents. Invoices and emails are what turn a claim into something enforceable. You can send them now.
  3. Your amount joins the total on the record, anonymously unless you tell us otherwise.
  4. You get the recovery tools — the small claims walkthrough, complaint language, and the filing details for your state.

What we do with your information

Your email address and phone number are used to contact you about this effort and nothing else. They are never published, never sold, and never shared with anyone outside this group without you specifically allowing it. Your name appears publicly only if you check the box asking us to list it.

Want your information removed? Email sam@crossroadstechnology.co and it is deleted. No questions, no delay.
